Why most AI output is average

What we usually do

  • Open a blank chat
  • Paste in a little context
  • Get something serviceable
  • Rewrite it ourselves anyway

What never makes it in

  • The document you actually use
  • The stories only you can tell
  • The way you actually write
  • Everything you said last time

Average in, average out. The fix isn't a better prompt.

Three things that change the output

It reads your real files

The template you actually use. Not a summary you retyped into a chat box.

It uses your real tools

Calendar, email, browser, documents — it goes and gets things, and puts things back.

It remembers you

You stop reintroducing yourself. Session ten knows what session one knew.

What just happened

It read the source

The actual posting and the actual profile — not my summary of them.

It found the opener

One true detail about a real person. That's a conversation, not flattery.

It picked the stories

Matched my existing stories to their themes. I didn't choose.

It kept my structure

My template, my voice, my format. Filled in, not replaced.

Do this yourself this week

Fifteen minutes of setup. Then it's yours, not mine.

1

Give it your raw material

Point it at a folder. Put in the document you rebuild every single time — the template, the checklist, the report format.

2

Write the instruction once

In your own words: what the document is for, what good looks like, what you always get wrong. Save it.

3

Run it on something real

Not a test case. Something actually due this week. You only learn where it fails on real work.
